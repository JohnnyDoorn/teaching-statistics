We’re going to be talking about something called moderation analysis, and moderation analysis is nothing more than looking at interactions between variables. If there is an interaction effect, you should always interpret your predictor’s main effects in the context of that interaction, but the difference now is that we’re going to be looking at continuous predictors, because in block 3, life is continuous. We’re going to look at interactions from the regression module, but we’re also going to look at how to analyze moderation effects using the Process module. Mediation is about your predictors being associated with each other, and that is a different thing from your predictors having an interaction with each other.
