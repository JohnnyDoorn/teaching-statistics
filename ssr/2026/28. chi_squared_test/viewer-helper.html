The topic for today is going to be the chi-squared test, which is all about quantifying an association between two categorical variables, the last combination of types of variables that we’ll see. How can we test the null hypothesis that says there is no association between your categorical variables, and what are some other things we can look at? We can do hypothesis testing to quantify evidence against a null hypothesis by means of a p-value, but we can also look at some effect sizes. And then, of course, I’ll show how to do all of this in JASP.
